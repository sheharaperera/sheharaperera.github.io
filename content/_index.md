My PhD work focuses on quantum algorithms for differential equations, with applications to computational mechanics. I am particularly interested in exploiting the structure of differential operators and numerical discretisations to design efficient quantum algorithms for large-scale scientific computing problems.

My previous research involved applied problems in robotics and vehicle dynamics. Through this work, I became increasingly interested in the mathematical structure underlying physical systems, leading me towards more fundamental questions in their modelling and simulation. I am particularly interested in developing computational methods that exploit this structure, and in exploring how emerging computational approaches can enable more efficient and scalable solutions to complex physical problems.

Previously, I was a Research Assistant at [Morph Lab](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/morph-lab/), Imperial College London. Prior to that, I received my BSc in Engineering from the [University of Moratuwa](https://www.uom.lk/), Sri Lanka.
